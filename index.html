How It Works

From first hello to final album

I've designed a process that is warm, personal and completely stress-free — so you can simply look forward to your session.

01

Initial Enquiry

Tell me about your family, your vision and when you're hoping to shoot. I'll come back to you within 48 hours to chat through the details.

02

Planning Together

We'll discuss location, outfits, timing and everything that makes the session feel like you — not a generic template.

03

Your Session

Relax and enjoy the time together. I'll guide you gently — no awkward posing, just natural moments as they happen.

04

Delivery & Keepsakes

Your edited gallery is delivered within 3 weeks. Prints, albums and wall art are available to order from your personal gallery portal.
